User Data Deletion Policy for Tap The Dot

If you wish to delete your data associated with the Facebook Instant Game "Tap The Dot", please follow the steps below:

  1. Go to your Facebook account settings.
  2. Select “Apps and Websites”.
  3. Find “Tap The Dot” in the list of apps.
  4. Click “Remove” to delete all associated data.

Alternatively, you can contact us at your-email@example.com with your Facebook User ID, and we will manually delete your game data within 7 working days.
